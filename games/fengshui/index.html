Feng Shui Planner

Works anywhere on Earth: set your location in the Sun tab (a built-in city list, or latitude/longitude and time zone), and the orientation of your plan in the Room tab. There's a compass helper for magnetic north. Sun paths, seasons and daylight follow your hemisphere. South of the equator you can optionally swap the north and south bagua palaces, as some modern schools do.

Describe a room, place furniture, and get an automatic reading based on traditional feng shui guidance, with suggested alternative layouts. Everything runs in this one file and is stored only in your browser and in files you save.

Describing the room

  • Room tab: the outline is a list of corner points (line vectors) in metres, x pointing east and y pointing north on the plan. Any polygon works, including L-shapes. Use Quick rectangle for a simple box.
  • Compass: “plan up bearing” is the compass direction the top of the plan faces. Leave at 0° if the top of your plan is north.
  • Doors & windows: attach doors, windows, open doorways and heaters to walls with an offset from the wall’s start corner. Drag them along the wall on the plan.
  • Furniture: add pieces from the library, drag them on the plan, and set the direction each one faces. For chairs, “faces” is the direction the sitter looks. For a desk, it is the side where you sit.

Keys

R/Shift+R rotate 90° · [/] rotate 15° · arrow keys nudge (Shift for bigger steps) · Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo · wheel zoom · drag the background to pan.

What the analysis checks

  • Command position: seats should see the door without being in line with it, with a solid wall (the “turtle”) behind rather than a window or door.
  • Chi flow: is the entrance (Ming Tang, the “bright hall”) open? Does chi rush straight from the door out of a window? Can every piece be reached with at least 60 cm clearance? Do long straight runs let chi race through the room like an arrow?
  • Sha chi: sharp corners and open shelves aimed at where you sit (“poison arrows”).
  • Five elements: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water by compass sector (Luo Shu nine palaces), the productive and controlling cycles, water next to fire, and overall balance.
  • Bagua life areas: compass school (Eight Mansions) by default, or entrance-aligned (BTB) under Room settings.
  • Kua number (optional): personal favourable directions for the way your seats face.

Feng shui is a traditional practice, not an empirical science. The rules here follow commonly taught guidance, simplified into checks a computer can run. Use them as prompts for thinking about the room, together with practical needs like light, plumbing and cabling.
