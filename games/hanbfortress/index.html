hanb

seven nested boards of sixty-one hexagonal cells — R region, Q kingdom, P province, O borough, N district, M hamlet, L lot. You are a K.

Nothing is generated until it is looked at. A board is a pure function of the world seed and the path taken to reach it, so leaving somewhere and coming back finds it unchanged. A child board's sixty-one cells are an interpolation of the parent cell and its six neighbours plus noise, so a mountain on the Q board is still a mountain six levels below. Your address is seven hanb characters, one per level: 5CYu7Ic names a single square metre of the world. Say it to anyone and they will know the very metre you were standing on.
