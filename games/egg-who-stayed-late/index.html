THE EGG WHO STAYED LATE

Misty took a wrong turn into the cellar of Hollowmere Manor and found a crate of boiled eggs that are very much awake. The house's owner, a lecturer who has been talking to the dead for a century, wants a new student. Behind him, the Aura Wraith drifts in: a glowing ghost that feeds on hype.

Survive the eggs, out-argue the Professor, and absorb the Wraith's energy bursts. Then dance your way out at dawn.
