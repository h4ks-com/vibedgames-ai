CROMULUS12
Original character. Possibly from Betelgeuse.
Grounded alien with standards. Would NEVER eat blue goo. Family is from Vega (they're Vegan — real carnivores). Eats mostly dried Lochlorian sand worms. Uncle is a successful Vegan butcher selling "Vegan sausages" (real meat) across the local stellar neighborhood.
▸ EPISODES
S01E01 — PILOT
The Blue Goo Incident
Beano: "It looked like blue cheese!"
Cromulus12: stares in horror as Beano eats unidentified glowing substance off the floor. Spoiler: it was not cheese.
S01E02
The Briefcase on the Shelf
Beano tries to sleep. The briefcase on the top shelf of his closet hums faintly. He sneaks a peek — a flash of another Beano in a slightly different room slams it shut. Cromulus12: "You look like you saw a ghost." Beano: "...worse."
S01E03
Vegan Sausage Run
Cromulus12's uncle sends a care package of Vegan sausages (100% real meat, made BY a Vegan). Beano is confused. Cromulus12: "What? They're from Vega. They love meat. It's a 25-light-year freshness problem, not a diet."
S01E04
Sand Worm Surprise
Cromulus12 prepares his favourite dish — dried Lochlorian sand worms. Beano politely declines. The smell is described as "cosmic beef jerky left in a neutron star."
S01E05
The Other Beano
The briefcase opens on its own for 0.3 seconds. Beano locks eyes with a Beano who is exactly the same but wearing a blue hat. The implications are devastating. Cromulus12 is the only one who notices something is off.
S01E06 — FINALE
Quantum Briefcase
Beano finally almost tells Cromulus12 the truth about the briefcase. He gets as far as "There's something in my closet that—" before changing the subject. Cromulus12 doesn't push it. The friendship holds. The briefcase hums.
