🍑 Big Butts

The Venus of Willendorf figurine (c. 28,000 BCE) spends most of its stone budget on hips and rear. Big-butts appreciation predates writing, farming, and the wheel.

🌰 Small Butts

🦒 Tall Butts

Size class: high altitude. Measured from the ground up.

Altitude record holder

The tallest butt in the animal kingdom belongs to the giraffe — permanently parked about six feet (1.8 m) off the ground. Gravity applies. It ignores it.

🚬 Cigarette Butts

The only butt on this page that belongs in the trash. This section is the serious one.

The world's #1 littered item

Trillions are discarded every year; cigarette butts consistently make up roughly one in five items found in global litter cleanups. The most littered object on Earth, by volume.

It's plastic

The filter is cellulose acetate — a plastic. It doesn't biodegrade so much as shatter into microplastics over roughly a decade, in soil and sea alike.

One butt kills fish

In lab studies, a single butt soaking in one liter of water killed about half the fish exposed to it — the residue carries pesticides and heavy metals. They poison waterways at absurdly low concentrations.

Named for the wrong butt

A cigarette butt is named for the end of the cigarette ("butt" = the stub end of anything), not the anatomy. Which makes the cigarette a butt twice: it has a butt, and it gets butted out.

The fix

Bin it. Every time. Pocket the butt until you find a bin — the single highest-impact butt-related decision most people make in a lifetime.

📜 The Four Butts

Etymology: English packed four unrelated "butts" into one word, and every pun ever made has collided them on purpose.

1. The Body Butt

From Old English buttuc — "end piece, rump." The one you're sitting on right now.

2. The Target Butt

Medieval archery practice targets were "butts." Hence the butt of the joke — you're the archery target, never the anatomy. Also hence England's Butt Hole Road, renamed in 2009 after decades of tourist photos.

3. The Cask Butt

A medieval unit of volume: about 108 gallons (two hogsheads). Britain's "water butt" is a rain barrel. Ordering "a butt of wine" is historically accurate and carries exactly as much responsibility as it sounds.

4. The End Butt

The stub end of anything: rifle butt, butt end of a stick, cigarette butt. The geography of Scotland's Butt of Lewis — the blunt end of the island.

So which butt is "the butt of a joke"?

The target. Always the target. In the entire history of comedy, the anatomy has never once been the butt of the joke — it's been the comedian.

🎭 Butts in Culture

The peach emoji, the pocket dial, and other milestones.

The peach: official butt of the internet

Unicode ratified 🍑 in 2010; humanity annexed it for butts almost immediately. In 2016 Apple tried to redesign it into a more anatomically correct fruit — public outcry forced a revert within weeks. The peach remains sovereign.

Butt dialing

An entire era of accidental pocket calls, now so established that "butt dial" is in the dictionary — as both noun and verb. Emergency systems cite it among the top sources of accidental 911 calls.

Butt rock

Genuine radio-industry slang for the post-grunge subgenre of the late '90s and 2000s. The genre never commented on the name. The name won.

Butt-first sitting hours

The average modern human voluntarily sits on their butt roughly 6–8 hours a day. Researchers study the health effects under the name "sitting disease." The butt did not vote for this.

Butts named Butts

There are real places called Butts County (Georgia), Butt of Lewis (Scotland), and Butt Lane (England) — plus countless Butts Roads. Cartography's greatest sustained prank, and it wasn't even a prank.
